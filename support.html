Stock Doctor evaluates 4 core vectors: Technical Trend (50 & 200 SMA, 14-day RSI), Momentum Volatility, Fundamentals (P/E relative to sector benchmarks), and Institutional Flows. A composite score out of 100 is generated. Scores above 75 receive BUY/STRONG BUY ratings with calculated entry zones, stops, and target prices. All ratings are algorithmic simulations for educational research.
Calculations follow the Union Budget FY2025-26 provisions:
  • STCG (Short-Term Capital Gains): Equity held for less than 12 months is taxed at 20%.
  • LTCG (Long-Term Capital Gains): Equity held for 12 months or longer is taxed at 12.5% on gains exceeding the ₹1,25,000 annual exemption limit.

When you click "Quick Trade" or "Order Bridge", the terminal formats a deep-link prefilled with the ticker, quantity, and suggested price. You are redirected to your broker app (Zerodha Kite, Groww, AngelOne, Dhan) where you must manually authenticate and confirm every order.
Price data, order book quotes, and indices are aggregated via DhanHQ Open APIs, Groww public feeds, and official AMFI mutual fund daily NAV releases. During active market hours (09:15 to 15:30 IST), live market ticks refresh automatically every 5 to 20 seconds.
The Mutual Funds engine computes the Jaccard Similarity index across the top 10 underlying stock holdings of your funds. High overlap (>60%) indicates that multiple funds hold identical companies, resulting in hidden concentration and duplicated expense ratios.
